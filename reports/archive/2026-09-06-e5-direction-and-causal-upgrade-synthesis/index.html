Abstract

RCoT-v2 is a solid E2 building block, and for E2 recovery the fashionable causal-orientation upgrade is mostly redundant — temporal order and causal sufficiency give it for free. But measured against the real goal E5 — does complete causal fan-out let a planner reject a locally-attractive-but-globally-harmful shared-knob action? — the next move is not a fancier discovery algorithm. It is (1) the never-run thesis end-to-end on E2, now shown to fire in prototype, hardened into a real pipeline, and (2) the multi-intervention world model (do-propagation on discovered structure, signed, later deconfounded) that the shared-knob decision actually needs. The genuinely useful discovery upgrades (tiered low-order skeleton; determinism-native functional support) and a GPU-accelerated frozen RCoT are safe and reusable, but not the critical path.

1. The headline, and the yardstick correction it depends on

The first (E2-scoped) synthesis judged “go causal” against E2 edge-recovery and answered “mostly redundant.” The second synthesis judged it against the END GOAL E5 and reached a different emphasis. Both are kept; the correction is the point.

  • E2-scoped verdict. For E2, the “genuine causality” machinery — PC/FCI orientation, colliders, v-structures, Markov-equivalence classes, and every FCM orientation method (ANM/PNL/LiNGAM/IGCI) — is largely REDUNDANT, because two properties E2 has for free remove the need: direction is known (temporal order t→t+1 orients every edge — the one thing every FCM method exists to provide) and causal sufficiency holds (params are independent exogenous roots; no latent confounders → FCI’s latent machinery is overkill). This verdict is correct for E2 recovery and is re-affirmed in §2.
  • E5-first correction. Judging against E2 recovery was the wrong yardstick for the project’s actual thesis. E5 is “does complete causal fan-out let a planner reject a locally-attractive-but-globally-harmful shared-knob action?” Against that, the real levers are completeness, signed interventional effects, deconfounding, and a multi-intervention world model — not orientation. This reframes, rather than contradicts, the E2 verdict.

A second explicit self-correction, on effect estimation: the first wave dismissed the user’s Tencent/RAPIDS links as “effect estimation, NOT structure discovery — wrong family.” The effect-quantification lane judged that E2-discovery-myopic: effect estimation is a first-class part of E5 (see §4). The sharper true reason those specific tools stay unadopted is regime (big-data confounded-ATE scalers vs a ≤14-node known-mechanism SCM), not family.

2. What “going causal” does and does NOT buy — per component

E2 structure (from specs + discovery_rcot.py): 8 exogenous mutually-independent params (DAG roots), 6 KPIs each a deterministic noiseless nonlinear (Gaussian-bump) function of a param subset; candidates per KPI target = 14 (8 params + 6 lagged KPIs); 16 true param→KPI edges; all 36 lagged KPI→KPI candidates are true-negatives (KPIs are pure functions of current params, no autoregression). Three graph facts dominate: every edge is oriented by time; no latent confounders; deterministic KPIs are terminal sinks.

Table 1. Per-component verdict for E2, grounded in E2/E5 structure.
ComponentVerdict for E2Reason
Skeleton phase — tiered low-order (PC-stable) conditioning The one real prize Removes all 68 true-negative cells at order 0 / low order (marginal RCoT is trivially calibrated + well-powered there), keeps the 16 true edges (a genuine parent has no separating set), and sidesteps the exact block_perm×BH resolution artifact that forced RCoT-v1→v2 — all with far less high-dim kernel regression than one full 13-D test per edge.
v-structure / collider / Meek / CPDAG orientation Buys nothing E2 is full of colliders (P_i→K←P_j) but all are already oriented by time; no undirected edge is left for Meek. CPDAG collapses to the true DAG once the skeleton is known.
FCI (latent confounders, PAG) Buys nothing for E2 Causal sufficiency holds. Relevant only as latent detection at E4/E5 — and even there gives a bidirected edge with no sign.
Error control Our per-target BH-FDR is the stronger story Plain PC is an α-cascade with no graph-level guarantee and is order-dependent (use PC-stable). Adopting a PC skeleton costs the clean BH-FDR guarantee unless FDR is re-added on the skeleton tests — a genuine trade-off, not a strict win.

Determinism — the honest caveat

PC/FCI assume faithfulness, which determinism violates (Y=f(S) ⇒ extra CIs not from d-separation). But the canonical failure (a determined node with a child gets its true out-edge wrongly deleted) does not arise on E2 — determined KPIs are terminal sinks. Empirically (companion record §2.3) determinism did not cost power on true edges (raw power ~80%; the tested parent is not in Z so its contribution survives residualization); it bit as co-parent KPI→KPI false positives (sibling KPIs share params), which a genuine conditional test controls. Score-based (GES) is not a drop-in for noiseless E2 (zero residual variance ⇒ degenerate BIC). The NeurIPS-2024 determinism-aware DGES and DPC are roadmap ideas for denser later envs, not E2.

The two upgrades that emerged (both new science → fresh pre-reg, truth-free prototype first)

Bar for both = beat RCoT-v2’s ~0.7 recall / ~0.01 FP on the byte-faithful test-bed with strictly lower-dim conditioning.

  • (A) Tiered PC-stable skeleton search over the frozen RCoT oracle (conditioning order ≤2–3), keeping per-target BH-FDR on top. Reference pattern: causal-learn (MIT, pluggable CIT, tiered background knowledge, Windows/NumPy-clean); the skeleton loop is ~40 lines — adopt the pattern, not the library. tigramite/PCMCI is the native time-series twin (GPLv3).
  • (B) Determinism-native functional-support / Markov-boundary discovery — exploit noiselessness: H(Y|S)=0 exactly when S ⊇ true parents. Subsumes RCoT; where it should win is the ~30% of true edges RCoT misses (low-amplitude / width-param edges) and robustness as later envs add real KPI→KPI edges. Non-starters ruled out with the violated assumption: ANM/PNL (need noise; degenerate at zero noise; produce orientation we have), LiNGAM (needs linearity), IGCI (bivariate orientation + invertible f; our KPIs are many-to-one), plain PC (harmed by determinism-faithfulness).

Supplied arXiv links, identified and judged off-target: 1705.09031 (MNAR test-wise-deletion FCI — we have no missingness; only genuine thread is that its author, Strobl, also authored RCoT); 1502.02454 (parallel-PC scalability — we have 14 nodes). Neither touches determinism.

3. Systems / GPU — the batched-RCoT plan and the staging rule

  • Both user links are effect-estimation, wrong for structure discovery. Tencent fast-causal-inference (DML/ATE over ClickHouse/StarRocks SQL) and the NVIDIA RAPIDS blog (cuML RandomForest inside DoubleML) learn effects given a graph, not the graph. Do not adopt for discovery.
  • GPU structure-discovery prior art = learn-from, not adopt. cuPC and gpucsl are GPU PC skeletons but hard-code a Gaussian partial-correlation CI test in CUDA (Fisher-Z — exactly RCoT’s blind spot), Linux/CuPy-only. cuPC GPL-3 stale; gpucsl MIT but Linux-only. Learn the parallelization scheme (pairs × conditioning-sets as batch axes).
  • Closest match = Bloomberg causal-ts (PyTorch, ships GPU-accelerated RCOT driving PC/PCMCI+), cross-platform → Windows-viable — but GPL-3.0-or-later (a genuine gate: reading for design is free, vendoring makes our tree GPL).
  • Adopt causal-learn (py-why, MIT, pluggable RCOT/KCI CI, p-value caching) as the CPU skeleton host / correctness oracle for a PC-with-RCoT prototype.
  • Build call: the object to batch on GPU is our RCoT (RFF residualization + block-perm null), not a stock Gaussian test, so build the batched GPU-RCoT in torch ourselves (fits 4 GB comfortably — RFF tensors ~4000×25; the embarrassingly-parallel 299-perm null is the ideal GPU workload and the current CPU bottleneck), keep the PC skeleton on CPU. Cloud GPU only for the full multi-seed sweep / E4–E5 blowup.
Staging rule (matches freeze discipline). (a) GPU-accelerate the existing frozen RCoT-v2 behind a device= flag with an equivalence gate (GPU masks == CPU masks, bit-for-decision) → behavior-identical ⇒ no re-freeze, pure systems; (b) the method upgrades (§2 A/B, PC skeleton) are new science ⇒ fresh pre-reg + adversarial review. Do (a) first — it de-risks (b). Determinism of the GPU oracle (pinned RNG, fixed reduction order / fp64 accumulation, documented device) is a freeze requirement, and validating GPU==CPU on E2 seed-0 is the single biggest correctness risk. Nondeterminism sources: GPU atomics/reductions + the RFF RNG.
GPU-port tension (flagged, unresolved by measurement). Plan 010 recommends against GPU-porting RCoT (tiny RFF dims, overhead-bound, already ~3–10 min/seed) — but that verdict is scoped to E2’s single-full-Z, 84-test design. A tiered skeleton search issues far more, lower-order CI tests, and E3/E5 want larger n / more reps under noise, which plausibly flips the economics toward batched GPU-RCoT as the oracle. Resolve by measuring test-count × per-test cost under the skeleton design, not under E2’s — do not inherit E2’s scoping by accident.

4. E5 = an effect-quantification-for-decision problem; the load-bearing blocker is the world model

4.1 What E5 is (grounded in SPEC.md:556-599)

E5 is the composition capstone — no new equations: an E2 shared-control fan-out block + an E3 temporal KPI→KPI chain + an E4 latent-Z confounding block + moderate observation noise ON, scored by aggregate AND worst-case regret, ID and OOD (common support-shift rule, SEMANTICS §7). Gating discipline: each sub-mechanism must pass its own SCM + decision-gap + control gate before E5; a collapse-under-composition is itself a legitimate integration-negative result.

The shared-knob decision is definitionally an effect-magnitude/sign question. E2’s decoy omits exactly one fan-out edge P0→K5 and therefore mispredicts do(P0=v)’s effect on K5 as zero — the trap is an effect-estimation error, not (only) a discovery failure (decoy and oracle score the identical panel; only the predicted K5 effect differs). “Globally harmful” = the v maximizing the incomplete-fan-out objective differs from the v maximizing the complete-fan-out one, because the omitted term flips the aggregate:

R = −[ Σ w_i · dist_i · s − (Σ ok_i)² ]

E3 sharpens the same shape across time; E5 adds worst-case + confounding.

4.2 The four things E5 needs — and the one blocker they all point at

  1. CI edge oracle — RCoT-v2. Have it (E2 solid).
  2. Completeness — recover the full oriented fan-out incl. real multi-hop lagged KPI→KPI chains first appearing at E3 (P0→K0→K1→{K2,K3}). RCoT was validated only to reject KPI→KPI, never to recover it — flagged as the sharpest unbuilt risk. Now DE-RISKED by the E3 chain-recovery study (recall 1.00 / precision 1.00 / 0 FP, both FULL and low-order conditioning; over-conditioning hazard refuted) — but only at E3’s noiseless operating point; re-validate under E5 noise (companion record §9).
  3. Signed interventional effects — RCoT is a test, not an estimator. E4 proves correlation gets the SIGN catastrophically wrong (pooled observational slope +3.29 vs true α=−1; a robust reversal, not knife-edge). Time does not orient this (both A and K_out same-transition-lagged); identification comes only from the randomized-interventional (do) subset. Needs structural do-propagation → a world model.
  4. Deconfounding — E4’s latent Z (never a param/KPI; observed adjacency only (K0,P0)), identified only from the do-subset. Least- built, highest-risk; a null is a legitimate boundary result (prior 5-seed deconfounding tests had all CIs including 0). Do not tune Z/λ upward to manufacture a gap.
The load-bearing blocker = a multi-intervention WORLD MODEL (do-propagation on discovered, possibly-confounded, multi-hop structure → signed KPI effects). Three lanes and three memory threads converge: cma-es-gate-falsified (block-covariance buys nothing; the +4.82 coordination win is oracle-only and model-OOD because the shipped model was fit on single-param interventions), planner-set-redesign-direction (“the planner is NOT the bottleneck … the real fix = world-model multi-intervention capability”), causal-planner-direction-explored. rollout.py already does complete-fan-out do-propagation on the TRUE simulator (the oracle answer); the missing piece is doing it on discovered/learned structure — extend the one-step learned predictor (cdl.py:640) to multi-step interventional rollout that chains predicted KPIs through discovered KPI→KPI edges. The fan-out quantification IS the world model the planner queries. QACM (and CID, which is deterministically ≡ QACM today) is a fixed market baseline (third-party), not “our” planner.

4.3 What “going causal” does NOT mean here (so we don’t chase fashion)

  • Orientation machinery is redundant THROUGH E5 — architecture-level fact: k_t=f(p_{t-1},k_{t-1}) (base.py:83,108) forbids within-step KPI→KPI edges, so all fan-out is lagged and time-orients for free, even at E3+. Colliders exist but are pre-oriented; no Meek step ever runs. The gap is completeness, not orientation.
  • FCI matters only as latent detection (bidirected edge, no sign); the sign comes from the interventional subset E4 provides, not from FCI. It is a flag, never the solver.
  • Determinism-native / ANM / LiNGAM / IGCI are redundant toward E5 — E5 turns noise ON (killing the determinism regime AND, with Gaussian Z/noise, the non-Gaussianity lever), and time already gives direction.
  • Big-data effect scalers (RAPIDS/DoubleML, Tencent) — right family, WRONG regime. They scale confounded ATE to millions of rows; we have a ≤14-node, known-mechanism, mostly-unconfounded SCM. The relevant reference is DoWhy-GCM (fit SCM + propagate do through the graph = exactly the fan-out quantification; arXiv:2206.06821) as a pattern (our simulator already hands us mechanisms and rollout.py already propagates — use it as the learned-arm estimator + a refutation-vocabulary harness, not a wholesale dependency). EconML/DML scoped to E4’s confounded block only (recover α=−1 against the +3.29 pooled slope), and even there identification leans on the randomized subset.

5. Build order (convergent across both waves)

  • Stage 0 (systems, now): GPU-accelerate the frozen RCoT-v2 (equivalence gate, no re-freeze). Resolve the plan-010 GPU-port tension by measurement against the skeleton design (§3).
  • Stage 1 — the missing spine: wire discovered-structure → world-model → planner → decision END-TO-END on E2 so the thesis is finally exercised. This has now been done as a DEV prototype and the thesis FIRED (companion record §5); the enduring build task is to replace the structure-ablation world model with the multi-step learned interventional world model on discovered structure (cdl.py:640 one-step → multi-step chaining).
  • Stage 2 (E3): build e3slice; recover the multi-hop KPI→KPI chain (the true-negative→true-positive flip — de-risked, recall 1.00 noiseless); tiered PC-stable low-order conditioning carried forward.
  • Stage 3 (E4): deconfounding via the interventional subset (EconML/DML there); signed effects.
  • Stage 4 (E5): noise-robust re-calibration of the CI null + BH resolution, composition, aggregate + worst-case ID/OOD scoring. No new mechanism may enter.
  • Critical path = completeness + signed-effects/world-model + confounding — NOT orientation, NOT determinism-exploitation. Joint/multi-param intervention prediction is what closes the OOD gap that keeps the +4.82 coordination win oracle-only today.
Do NOT add benchmark noise to E2 to “simulate” E5 — counter-productive, risks a gameable benchmark (Reisach et al., “Beware of the Simulated DAG!”, arXiv:2102.13647), and disables the sharp determinism-native signal. Make the method noise-ready; let E5 turn noise on via its config sweep as specified.

6. Bottom line

RCoT-v2 is a solid E2 building block, and for E2 recovery the fashionable causal-orientation upgrade is mostly redundant (time + sufficiency give it free). But measured against the real goal E5, the next move is not a fancier discovery algorithm. It is (1) the never-run thesis end-to-end on E2 — now shown to fire in prototype — hardened into a real pipeline, and (2) the multi-intervention world model (do-propagation on discovered structure, signed, later deconfounded) that the shared-knob decision actually needs. Discovery feeds it; it is the lever three prior threads already named. The genuinely useful discovery upgrades (tiered low-order skeleton; determinism-native functional support) and the GPU-accelerated frozen RCoT are safe, reusable, but not the critical path.
