E-series learned-arm de-risks & the E2 single-harm design decision

This report records the 2026-09-07 layer on top of the E2 empirical record: two learned-arm de-risks for the E-series decision pipeline (the objective-function fixes and E4 deconfounding), and the design decision that keeps E2 a single-harm atom. The through-line across all three is that discovery-completeness, not the objective or the planner, is the E-series lever — the objective fixes buy nothing here, while the deconfounding route that matters is an interventional-identification one, not an objective knob. Everything below is a DEV prototype or truth-free analysis; nothing frozen is changed.

Two null / feasibility results and one design decision. (1) The legacy objective fixes FM-1/FM-2 do NOT transfer to the E-series — a pre-declared λ-sweep never improves realized R. (2) E4 deconfounding is a VIABLE but finite-sample-fragile identification route: the do-subset recovers α=−1 against a sign-flipped +3.30 pooled slope. 1. FM-1 / FM-2 objective re-validation on the E-series — NULL

Completes plans/011 §2 and plans/012 step-1.

Re-validation of the legacy objective-function fixes on the LIVE E-series objective (v2_regret.py / counterfactual_metrics.py), truth-free, pre-declared λ grid. The legacy magnitudes were Env I–IV; this tested whether they transfer.

  • FM-1 (flat-objective / utility_weight) does NOT transfer. The flat-hinge structure the fix targets IS present on the E-series (13/64 committed states have a flat R-optimal plateau with z-utility spread), but adding the utility_weight z-utility term never improves realized R across λ ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5}:
    • perfect world-model: λ=0 already realizes the oracle (norm_regret 0.0); λ>0 flat then mildly harmful (realized_pos −42.333 → −42.379).
    • decoy world-model: λ=0 regret 0.19; λ>0 strictly worse (−57.48 → −58.01).
    The legacy Env I gain (−0.083 → +0.303 at λ=0.25) does not reproduce. Mechanism: E-series realized R IS the same clamped hinge (over-satisfaction is worth zero realized value by construction), and the decision arm uses an analytic true/decoy world-model, not a learned/noisy ensemble whose error a satisfaction margin could buffer.
  • FM-2 (OOD-trust penalty) is NOT measurable on the current E-series decision arm: the ensemble-disagreement signal requires an ensemble world-model, which the single analytic true/decoy arm does not provide. Not built (user-gated module). Same “world-model capability, not an objective knob” conclusion as FM-3.
Consequence. plans/011 FM-1/FM-2 magnitudes are legacy-only — must not be credited to the E-series. The option-a-utility-weight-validated memory is caveated accordingly. This completes the plans/012 step-1 gate as a null, and reinforces the night’s through-line: discovery-completeness, not the objective/planner, is the E-series lever. (Prototype: scratchpad fm_eseries/.)

2. E4 deconfounding feasibility — VIABLE route

Feasibility only; the scored gate arm remains user-gated.

Truth-free feasibility check on the E4 DGP (e4.py): mechanism K_out = α·A + θ·Z + c with α=−1, θ=+2.5, latent Z~N(0,1) driving BOTH the clipped behavior policy A = clip(0.5 + λ·Z + η) (λ=1) AND the outcome → observational correlation is sign-flipped; do() draws A ⊥ Z, breaking the backdoor. 10 seeds:

Table 1. E4 estimator comparison (10 seeds). Correlation flips the sign; the do-subset recovers it.
estimatorslope on A→K_out95% CIverdict
correlational pooler (naive K~A on 90:10 pool) +3.3046 [+3.29, +3.32] reproduces frozen +3.2916 sign reversal (wrong sign)
deconfounded (K~A on D=1 interventional subset) −1.0423 [−1.201, −0.883] CI excludes 0, recovers true α=−1
oracle backdoor (K~A+Z; Z latent, reference) −1.0000 — confirms {Z} is a valid backdoor
Honest caveat. Per-seed deconfounded slope is noisy (−0.69…−1.45, sd 0.26) because the D=1 residual is θ·Z (var 6.25) over only ~2000 do-rows/seed; robustness comes from the multi-seed mean. This under-power explains the prior 5-seed “not significant” null (causal-planner-direction-explored memory) — the identification route is SOUND, just finite-sample fragile at low seed/n. The scored E4 gate arm + matched corpus module remain user-gated (GATE_CONTRACT_E4 defers them). (Prototype: scratchpad e4_feas/.)

3. E2 design decision — keep the single-harm atom; place the method-breaker at E5

User-approved.

Trigger: verification that E2’s mechanism equations (e2.py) are a byte-for-byte copy of the retired legacy env_ii.py (adjacency identical); only the decoy (omit P0→K5), the operating-point tuning (P6 width), and the latency/tape infra are E2’s own. E2 harms exactly ONE service (K5), while the design table says “several harmed xApps”; SPEC.md:241 had been trimmed to “omits EXACTLY ONE edge P0→K5” (spec matched the code, not the table).

Decision (after an 8-lens debate — purist / conservative / construct-validity / adversarial-realism / minimalism / discovery-difficulty / falsificationist / composition-first): keep E2 as the clean single-harm atom. Do not rebuild, do not add several harms to E2.

  • Rationale: E5 is built by composing E2+E3+E4; the “several interacting traps” are meant to emerge from composition, and a single-axis E2 preserves failure-localization (if composed E5 breaks, you can attribute the failure). Matches SPEC.md “one stress axis per env” + “E5 = composition only, no new mechanism.” Avoids churning a frozen/validated artifact and the cascade risk (gold-plating every stepstone → E5 never ships).
  • On provenance: 7 of 8 lenses judged “independence from Env II” the wrong variable — no result can see where the equations were authored. The user’s deeper instinct (E2 should honestly be its own thing) is served by fixing FRAMING, not rewriting working equations.
  • Honesty fix — DONE, committed ce426f5: added a “single harmed xApp is intentional, not a shortfall” design note to the SPEC.md E2 section (doc-only; no behavior/constant/gate change).
  • Integrity requirement, and where it goes (DECIDED): the falsificationist + discovery-difficulty lenses require the benchmark to contain a trap our new stratified-recovery method CANNOT crack — a ≥2-knob joint gate — else we grade ourselves to pass. Placement: at E5, as the E2×E4 interaction, not in E2. E5 already composes E2’s shared knob with E4’s action/confounder, so a jointly-gated harm is an interaction of validated parts (spec-legal) rather than a new mechanism (which the spec would reject); it is also exactly what E5 exists to test. Caveat for E5-build: the composition must be deliberately wired to produce the joint gate (it won’t by accident) and pre-committed that the current method is expected to struggle there (no re-tune). Only if composition genuinely can’t produce it without a new equation do we face the fork (new env vs charter change). Not built yet.
  • Deferred, non-blocking (own conversation): the adversarial-realism lens flagged that the whole benchmark is abstract exp(−x²) math with zero O-RAN grounding (no transmit power / PRB / scheduling). Real external validity would need grounding in an actual O-RAN trade-off; applies to E1–E5 equally.
