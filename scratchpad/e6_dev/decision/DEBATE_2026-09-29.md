# Strategy debate after E6-P DEAD / xTRUCE v1 not screenable (2026-09-29)

Three independent read-only agents argued fixed positions. Each memo is condensed here, with its citations kept.

## A: "Noise is fundamental, so move to the slow timescale" (and its strongest counter)
### Evidence
Across 3 plants and 2 decision units, the failure is the same:
- **E6 region-policy:**
  - within-slot SD is 45.5, about the size of the headroom median of 43.6;
  - per-row SNR is about 0.006;
  - with noise-free oracle labels, CV R² is ≤ 0 and ρ = 0.076 (RANK_DIAG_RESULT.md:7-20).
- **E6 request-level:** whether a decision matters is predictable (AUC 0.97), but its sign is not (0.55-0.62).
- **E6-P:** ρ_sign is 0.547 / 0.607 / 0.658.

### What ρ_sign implies
- Model the advantage as N(μ, σ). Then P(same sign on redraw) = p² + (1−p)², with p = Φ(μ/σ). This gives
  **μ/σ ≈ 0.41 / 0.59 / 0.78**: even the oracle's chosen deviations have a mean below one noise SD.
- The oracle breaches guardrails. That fits selection on tape-specific futures (its advantage is the maximum over
  noise).

### The signal is at the slow timescale
- Aggregates are stable: the stage-1 loss has LB90 of 25-56, and it depends on regime (present at L40, absent at L10).
- If about 24 same-sign deviations per episode are pooled, regime-level μ/σ is about 0.6·√24 ≈ 2.9, which gives a
  sign stable about 99 % of the time.
- Djidjev & Kaminski say the same (E6_PUBLISHED_BASELINES.md:232): dependency tracking is for "diagnosis and slow-loop
  refresh, not autonomous mitigation".

### Pivot
**Claim.** *Regime-conditional causal co-deployment governance.* Per slow regime (load bin × surge state), a policy
chooses a continuous xApp envelope:
- activity per xApp;
- PowerES max step;
- ES sleep threshold and dwell;
- SG min share.

The envelope is learned from randomized regime-level interventions under CRN, with a signed causal effect model of
indirect harm. It maximises protected-slice SLA at matched energy under guardrails, and it outputs attributions of the
form "xApp → harmed KPI | regime".

**Why an envelope and not a subset.** Dropping PowerES recovers R ≈ 0.55 but fails energy retention. A partial,
regime-conditional envelope is the missing middle.

**Benchmark.**
- Plant: E6-P, cells P1 s3 and P3 s1/s3, with L10 as the "do nothing" control.
- Arms:
  - AA;
  - QACM;
  - PACIFISTA (δ_TOL 0.25 / 0.5, per load bin);
  - hindsight static subset;
  - hindsight static envelope per regime (the ceiling).

**Gate.**
- R ≥ … at retention ≥ 0.90, with RLF and LL ≤ 1.10×.
- An edge of ≥ 10 pp over the best eligible PACIFISTA/QACM arm.
- Regime-decision sign reliability ≥ 0.90.
- Secondary: signed attribution precision and recall versus CRN knockouts.

**Why it would beat the three published methods.**
- QACM is identical to AA here (one writer per knob).
- PACIFISTA uses unsigned ECDF distances and all-or-nothing decisions, so its ceiling is the static subset, which is
  ineligible here.
- xTRUCE's per-epoch convex projection lacks the chain sleep → neighbour load → protected PRB.

**Kill and cost.**
- Kill if no eligible regime envelope reaches R ≥ 0.35 in any L40 cell.
- Kill if the learned policy is no better than the hindsight static envelope − 5 pp.
- Collection costs about 11 CPU-h.

### Strongest counter
At regime scale the decision becomes a small lookup table. A DEV-tuned static envelope per regime is near-optimal by
construction. Any edge would then come from the richer action space, not from causal discovery; reviewers would call
it "PACIFISTA with better knobs". E5 already found that MSCR only *ties* DEV-tuned SHAP-GBDT on detection
(E5_NOISE_DETECT_RESULT.md). It is also not yet shown that any eligible envelope exists.

## C: "The rigorous negative results ARE the contribution" (and its strongest counter)
### Claims
- **C1. With one writer per knob, static priority, lock and QACM all equal accept-all.**
  - R_static is 0.00, 0.09 and 0.008, yet the loss is real (Λ 0.72-1.55, LB90 25-56).
  - Mechanism: QACM and priority resolve same-knob contention, and never see the WG3 ConMit §4.2.1 indirect class.
- **C2. Even a privileged per-decision oracle's headroom is mostly noise plus guardrail-breaking.**
  - Raw R is 0.38 / 0.88 / 0.65, reached only by over-sleeping (energy retention 1.14-1.36; RLF up to 1.39×).
  - ρ_sign is 0.55-0.66 over about 240 deviations per cell.
  - This replicates E6: oracle reliability 0.56, learned ρ of 0.01-0.04, sign AUC of 0.55-0.62.
- **C3. Published gains are measured against the wrong baseline.**
  - xTRUCE compares only to Direct and Clipping, PACIFISTA to no coordination, QACM to NSWF. None reports the best
    static rule (ENV_SCOUT.md).
  - The only static option that recovers anything here is "don't co-deploy", which is not energy-matched. Nobody
    reports it.
- **C4. Gate A v2 is a methodological contribution.** It is a preregistered four-criterion screen with a mechanism
  stage. It killed 3 benchmarks before any learner was trained. Criterion 4 (ρ_sign) is new and transferable.

### Release, venue and paper pieces
- **Release.** E6, E6-P and the xTRUCE plant, with their tests; hashed protocols; the seed registry; every arm; the
  screen drivers; raw jsonl including per-deviation redraws; the numeric fingerprint. Confirmation seeds stay

- **Venue.** A TNSM-style methods/benchmark paper, preprinted on arXiv now. Working title: "Does xApp Conflict

  Not as a section of the Discover paper, because it contradicts that paper's thesis.
- **Tables T1-T8.** Most are derivable from existing runs:
  - T5 and T6 from e6p-s2/summary.json;
  - T4 from RANK1_SUMMARY, PILOT_RESULT and RANK_DIAG;
  - T2 from GATE_A_RESULT.txt;
  - T3 (E6 Gate B) is partial: 17 of 24 jobs.
- **Figures F1-F6.** None exist yet. The key one is F4, the per-deviation true vs redrawn advantage scatter, from the
  `deviations` field in e6p-s2/all.jsonl.
- **Strongly advised before journal submission:** a *guardrail-aware* oracle, preregistered, costing about 30 CPU-h.
  Without it, C2 rests on the oracle's frozen, guardrail-blind objective. **It needs the user**, because the
  confirmation range 150200-150399 is untouched or reserved.

### Risks
- **The Discover draft.** sn-article.tex is still a skeleton titled "Causal Discovery-Driven Model-Based Planning".
  Its CDL claims come from diagnostic environments. Either narrow Discover to structure recovery with known ground
  truth and report E6 briefly, or drop its realism claims. Never put the E6-P tables in both papers.
- **Reviewer attacks:**
  - the plants are self-built;
  - "QACM ≈ AA" looks tautological, when it is actually the finding;
  - the M4 amendment came after outcomes (disclosed);
  - the 0.70 ρ_sign bar looks arbitrary, so report the continuum;
  - 8 seeds per stratum;
  - the oracle is guardrail-blind;
  - the "you couldn't build it" framing.

### Strongest counter
A null result on self-built plants, with an oracle objective we chose ourselves, says little about real RANs.
- C2 is partly self-inflicted: the oracle *did* recover 38-88 %.
- xTRUCE was never actually tested.
- Only C3 survives cleanly, and that is a critique, not a paper.
- Without the guardrail-aware confirmation, this is likely an IEEE Access-tier paper.

## B: "The kill reflects flaws in the oracle and estimator. Run E6-P v2." (and its strongest counter)
### Re-analysis of e6p-s2/all.jsonl (post hoc)
- **The oracle optimised a different problem from the one the gate scores.**
  - `Oracle.roll` returns only (prot_viol, viol_ue_s, energy ≤ bound) (e6p_screen.py:421-423). RLF and LL are
    invisible to it.
  - Its energy bound is one-sided (:404-408).
  - So it was ineligible by construction: a misspecified upper bound, not an empirical finding about headroom.
- **Some expected advantage survives a redraw.** The summed redrawn advantage (AA − plan, protected UE-s) is positive
  in all three cells:

  | cell | redrawn advantage | seed-bootstrap 5 % LB | share of true-tape advantage kept |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | P3 surge | +4112 | +1256 | 58 % |
  | P3 base | +1129 | +396 | 22 % |
  | P1 surge | +1490 | −320 | 48 % |

- **True-tape vs redrawn advantage correlate at 0.66 / 0.26 / 0.73** (P1, P3 base, P3 surge).
  - Signal SD is about equal to noise SD.
  - Selecting the best of about 29 single-tape rollouts builds in a winner's curse.
  - Sign retention weighted by |advantage| is 0.75 / 0.84 / 0.68. This is post hoc and cannot become a criterion.

### Proposed E6-P v2 (one shot, fresh seeds; v1 published as is)
1. **Guardrail-constrained oracle.**
   - Add AA per-second guardrail traces (RLF, LL, SVR, non-protected eMBB).
   - A plan is admissible iff every guardrail is ≤ 1.10 × AA at t + H.
   - Energy becomes **two-sided**: retention in [0.90, 1.10].
2. **Two oracles.**
   - O_tape is clairvoyant and serves only as a kill gate.
   - **O_exp** selects on the CRN mean over K = 4 redrawn futures (no tape lookahead) and is scored on the true tape.
     It is the "perfect world model" and CDD's real target. Criteria 2-4 are judged on O_exp.
3. **ρ_sign on K′ = 4 held-out futures** (the sign of the mean advantage), plus a seed-bootstrap LB of the summed
   held-out advantage > 0.
4. **RB_WINDOW = 120 s**, so the ES rollback is no longer inert, with a mechanism check.

**Unchanged:** the plant, strata, arms and all thresholds.
**Scope:** only the 3 cells from v1, re-gated on fresh seeds.
**Seeds:** B proposed 160000-160399, but that **clashes with the ARBITER_DESIGN block** (160000-179999, unregistered).
Pick another block.

**Cost.**

| stage | content | CPU-h |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | fresh-seed stage 1 | ~5 |
| 2a | O_tape | ~36 |
| 2b | O_exp (124 rollouts per epoch) | ~121 |
| 3 | hindsight static | ~10 |

The total is about 170 CPU-h, or about 41 CPU-h if killed at K2.

**Kill criteria.**
- **K1:** c1 fails in all 3 cells on the fresh seeds.
- **K2:** O_tape is ineligible or has R < 0.5 in every cell. Stop.
- **K3:** O_exp fails c2, c3, ρ ≥ 0.70 or LB > 0 in every cell.
- **K4:** no v3, ever.

**Forking paths, admitted plainly.** Every change was chosen after v1 failed. The two-sided energy band is the most
outcome-shaped.

### Strongest counter, in B's own numbers
Scale raw R by the share of advantage kept on a redraw:
- P1: 0.38 × 0.48 ≈ **0.18**
- P3 base: 0.88 × 0.22 ≈ **0.19**
- P3 surge: 0.65 × 0.58 ≈ **0.38**

All three are below the 0.50 bar, even before the guardrail constraints cost anything. Under a Gaussian fit, even
K → ∞ puts the share of deviations with positive expected advantage at only 0.63 / 0.67 / 0.70. The most likely v2
outcome is therefore DEAD at about 170 CPU-h. If it is run at all, run only O_tape (~36 CPU-h) first, as a cheap
falsifier.
