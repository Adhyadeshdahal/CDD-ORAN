## Notes (facts from the records and tables; no new numbers)

- **Declaration.** Commit 86a9767 on feat/v2; the same file was added on xm/exp-b in fee5171. Protocol LF sha256
  2dc21fac... (recorded in extras_tables.json and in every record's `integrity.extras`). Amendments: none.
- **All 7 declared specs ran in full.** 18,240 units; 0 missing, error or infeasible; one platform per spec;
  pinned packages match; Python 3.12.14. All 364 cells have status ok, and no cell is dropped.
- **NA entries.** These come from the frozen pipeline; none is a gap in the runs.
  - pcorr_eq at 5 setpoint blocks: on every record the frozen pcorr arm stops with "STOP: pcorr: rank-deficient
    source design". Every candidate is therefore not testable and is left out of every denominator, and recall is 0.
  - E4 R3 truth-null rates: the frozen eval_analysis returns no truth-null rate for this cell because its
    denominator is empty. The P_placebo and P_placebo_conf rates are reported.
- **Trend words** follow the declared rule, applied to the unrounded point estimates. Rounded to three decimals,
  two estimates can print equal even when the unrounded values differ.
- **X3 expected invariances held.** pcorr_eq's pooled rates are identical to the exact design under every width
  and every lambda variant, and they change under both switch-time variants. The i.i.d. P_placebo raw rate in E4
  R3 is identical across the lambda variants for every arm, so it works as the declared control.
- **Provenance platform.**
  - `extras analyse` ran on Linux: VPS /opt/cdd-xm, inside a systemd scope, using the pinned py 3.12.14 venv.
  - The code was the extras bundle at 99fdb05, which is identical to HEAD for `cdd_oran/` and the specs.
  - The frozen `eval_analysis.py` was uploaded from HEAD (sha256 73685abb...). The log is `analyse_vps.log`.
  - On Windows, the frozen generator itself does not reproduce some Linux datasets bit-for-bit: E2, E4, and E1
    seed 3200028. See `provenance_platform.json`. Every record was generated on Linux (Kaggle or VPS).
- **R-30 labels** in the tables are descriptive, not verdicts.
