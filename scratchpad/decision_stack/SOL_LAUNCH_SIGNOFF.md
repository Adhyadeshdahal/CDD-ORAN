# E6 DEV collection launch sign-off

## Policy grid (`e6-collect-v3`): **conditional GO**

The 12 fit-seed pilot pairs pass reported reconstruction, propensity, first-stage, continuation, washout and reference checks; its cost trigger did not fire. Network-wide 20 s treatment then 70 s accept-all covers interference neighbourhoods, excludes later assignments from H=90 labels, and matches the oracle. Lock tails into washout belong to the effect. Six labelled slots/episode reduce power versus v2.

**Before remaining jobs:** record pooled branch-frequency and per-xApp mode-marginal comparisons against F2's four-binomial-SD limit. The pilot explicitly did *not* compute this, so F2 is undocumented. If it fails, investigate and version/re-pilot; if it passes, freeze the draft contract and proceed with 180 registered episodes and paired refs (counting pilot seeds). Keep fit/diagnostic seeds separate. Reserved independent calibration/evaluation, `episode_max` and component guardrails precede any controller **claim**.

**Eligibility: approve treatment-window counting, with a caveat.** Requests during [start, +20 s) measure *realized first stage*; start-only gives zero TS support. But eligibility is post-assignment: policy can alter later requests. The 20/20/10 rule is an operational support gate, **not** causal positivity or an unbiased eligible-subgroup effect. Fit intention-to-treat on all randomized slots; report both eligibility definitions, no-ops/NACKs and episode counts. Never select fitting rows by window eligibility. Keep failing MRO/ES/modes unidentified; probes cannot fill them.

## Probe grid (`e6-probe/2`): **NO-GO until its own audit/freeze**

Four local replays show CIO/carrier persistence. The predeclared 30-job fit-seed carryover audit has not been reported. Run it **before** 180 main jobs; publish first-stage/restoration, lag-1, reference-placebo, effective blocks/episodes, aborts and paired costs. Investigate unexpected placebo rejection or actuation failure, then freeze the probe contract. Replays never enter inference. Design changes require versioning and re-audit.

**Randomization: retain blocks, claim only the whole-trajectory sharp null.** Re-drawing logged, outcome-independent assignments tests no family effect *anywhere in the episode*, even with carryover under the alternative. Rejection supports an assigned-family effect on a KPI trajectory, **not** a window-local edge or isolated knob effect. Report CIO/carrier contamination alongside declarations; graph remains a soft prior. `randomization="episode"` currently draws one A arm and one B arm per episode, not selectively CIO/carrier; switching requires a revised contract, fresh audit and episode-level power calculation. It does not erase carryover.

**Length: approve 1800 scored seconds for probes versus policy's 600.** Long slots and strict isolation require time; do not pool the different estimands. Charge the full duration and 1200-changes/hour cap (600 worst-case changes/episode) in paired SVR, slices, RLF, severe, energy, churn and changed-knob-time. Extra seeds require the blinded tranche rule, never effect signs or p-values.
