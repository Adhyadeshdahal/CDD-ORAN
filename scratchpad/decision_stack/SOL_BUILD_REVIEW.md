# Overnight decision-stack build: pre-collection verdict

**Do not launch the full 180+180 grid yet.** V2 records assignments and realised steps; `half` now has a meaningful first stage; the probe is separate from WG3. But the labels may not identify the model's **declared** estimand.

## BLOCKING before the cloud grid

1. **Policy continuation mismatch.** `collect_episode_v2` rerandomizes every 20 s; a 90 s label includes four later own-region assignments and neighbours' assignments. `PolicyEffectModel` uses own `pol_fut_*` only as additive effects, which cancel in candidate-minus-accept-all contrasts. Later policies interact with initial locks, carriers, KPM and xApp reactions. Data identify “initial policy, then logging policy,” not necessarily `TrueSimWM(continuation="accept_all")`'s “initial policy, then accept-all.” Predeclare **one**: preferably randomized 20 s treatment at spaced slots followed by ≥70 s accept-all in the region *and interference neighbourhood*, using only slot starts as H=90 labels; or target logging-policy continuation and evaluate that same stochastic continuation in the oracle. The first needs more episodes. Future main-effect covariates alone do not solve this.

2. **Probe null needs a schedule-level audit.** `crt.py` resamples assignments with outcomes fixed, valid for a *sharp null across the relevant trajectory*. A 30 s A washout or 60 s B washout may not erase queues, carrier dwell or xApp memory; later block outcomes may depend on earlier arms. B restores at 180 s, leaving 60 s before the next slot—less than carrier's 120 s dwell. Run paired same-seed **schedule replays** varying early probes, measuring later-block contamination and restoration/actuation by family; test sham-placebo rejection on the planned grid. If carryover survives, lengthen slots or randomize/analyze whole-episode schedules. Synthetic type-I calibration is not E6 validation.

3. **Predeclare support, costs and stopping.** Version the `StepMixture`, `quiet_epochs`, `D/H`, probe config, RNG tags `7708/8808/5151/6262` and seeds. Report first stage and paired collection cost, including abort/no-op blocks. The probe cap is **3600 changes/hour** (up to 600 in ten scored minutes), far above E6's old 300 writes/hour; defend this as a costed offline experiment and report excess SVR, slices, RLF, severe, energy and churn. Run a small **fit-seed-only** pilot across six strata to verify traces, propensities, compliance, caps/aborts and CRT placebo. Fix failure criteria first; protect diagnostic seeds.

4. **Calibration/evaluation split is unfunded.** Ten untouched policy episodes/stratum cannot both calibrate a per-stratum gate (`min_units=30`) and evaluate it independently. Default `method="pooled"` treats dependent epochs as exchangeable. Predeclare `episode_max` with disjoint calibration/evaluation **episodes**; pool strata only for a pooled claim, allocate more non-TEST seeds for conditional claims. Calibrate the actual search/plan class and separately bound guardrails.

## Worker K issue 1: keep strict units, buy independent episodes

**Keep strict isolation.** CIO-neighbour units overlap; load-coupled interference can extend beyond one edge. Accept 0.5-SD power ~0.5–0.7 or predeclare an independent **probe-only** tranche of 30 extra seeds/stratum (60 total), triggered solely by blinded effective-block/first-stage or simulated-power diagnostics, never observed p-values/signs. Assign new seeds beforehand and charge probe cost. Otherwise report `not_detected`/`undetermined`, not “no edge.” Do not substitute `discovery.py`'s row-permutation p-values for `mscr-crt-v1`.

## MRO/ES support: decide before data

At 30 episodes × 30 epochs × ~10 regions per stratum, **MRO eligibility 0.6% ≈54 region-epochs**, only ~7 assigned reject and ~7 half; ES 6% ≈540, ~72 each. Locks (~1.25% per xApp) and particular joint modes have still thinner support. Predeclare per xApp×scenario×load×mode counts of *eligible-at-epoch-start* requests, assigned policy, ACK/realised movement and independent episodes, plus a minimum-support rule (for example ≥20 eligible treated and ≥20 eligible accept-all in ≥10 independent episodes per stratum). Below it, exclude that xApp/mode from learned candidate scoring via the support gate and label its policy effect **unidentified**; no synthetic MRO/ES controller win. The operator probe can identify knob-family effects but cannot fill the missing *MRO/ES request-policy* support or model their NACK reactions. If gate A/B scenarios raise request frequency, let the registered counts decide; do not retune xApps or select favourable seeds.
