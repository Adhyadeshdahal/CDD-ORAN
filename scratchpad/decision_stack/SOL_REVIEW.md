# Adversarial review: E6 decision stack

**Verdict:** The new `cdd_oran/decision/` boundary and common search with swappable evaluators are right. Retiring single-knob legacy planners from the *E6 product path* is right. But `TrueSimWM` and `LocalResponseWM` do not evaluate the same intervention. Respect `decision/DECISION.md`: no model training until the corrected, budgeted WG3 oracle and scenario pass.

## BLOCKING — fix before interpreting a learned-controller result

1. **Define the estimand at the policy boundary, not “KPI after a knob changed.”** `wg3_oracle.py` chooses a *region-wide policy* over requests, locks and rollbacks for 20 s, scores 90 s of violated UE-seconds, and consumes churn. V1 predicts a 20 s per-cell KPI delta from immediate knob changes, ignoring future requests. Plans with identical immediate changes but different locks then become indistinguishable; 20 s misses ES dwell, MRO cadence and handovers. Define candidate-minus-accept-all *policy effects*, conditioned on observable history, on SVR components, RLF/severe, energy, goals and churn. Cover delayed effects or measure the residual. Require a same-state, same-future-tape oracle ablation of short-window candidate ranking and regret before adopting v1.

2. **The logs cannot train that estimand.** `E6Env.log` records only `t`, post-apply `config`, delivered `reports`, and `n_req` (`env.py:203–204`): no request values, decisions, pre-action configuration, ACK/NACK, locks, rollbacks, actual deltas, cap remaining or scored SLA increments. KPM p5 throughput/p95 delay are *not* violated UE-seconds. Add a versioned propose/apply/outcome trace, counter-based outcome windows and randomized-policy propensities. Runtime features must come from `obs`/past logs; privileged counters are **labels**. Split by episode/tape/scenario, not cell-seconds.

3. **Matched “no-change” is not a counterfactual.** Actions depend on KPM/load/requests; unchanged cells are exposed to neighbours' actions. Randomize feasible *joint request-policy perturbations* by region/epoch (accept-all, partial modify, lock, rollback), stratified by congestion, xApp/version and action size. Log probabilities and application success; account for dwell/churn. Fit candidate-minus-incumbent returns or sequential transitions with network-wide context. Rejection/lock can have zero immediate delta yet different futures. Audit action coverage and regret on oracle-scored, held-out candidate panels. D1/D2 already falsified passive level prediction.

4. **xApp reactions are first-order dynamics, not optional v2.** `xapps.py` uses delayed reports, cadences, EWMA, ES/SLICE timers, NACK-dependent retry/escalation/hold, threshold variants and version updates. `P(request | KPM state)` omits configuration, pending responses, proposal timing and report age. Use a learned **history-conditioned** request-and-plant transition model for long horizons, or restrict v1 to a validated one-epoch task. Test rollout drift after reject/lock and under updates; keep hidden simulator state out of deployable inputs.

5. **The confidence gate has no coverage guarantee.** Three MLP members' spread (`learned_world_model.py` returns only their mean) is not a confidence interval. Selecting the best of many candidates amplifies optimism. Calibrate *paired action-difference errors* by held-out scenario/support, including feasibility and selection bias; abstain to a declared fallback outside support. Report coverage, false-improvement and closed-loop guardrail violations under shift. Reward bounds do not guarantee slice/energy/retention constraints; bound those separately.

6. **Do not let the graph decide by omission.** Observational knob→KPI p-values are not intervention effects. Missing an edge could auto-accept harmful requests; own-KPI harm and interference matter too. Use the graph first as a *soft feature prior*; compare pruned/unpruned search with an oracle false-negative audit and rescue candidates. Claim causal value over SHAP/all-input only if held-out interventions and shifted-regime **paired decision regret** favour MSCR at equal capacity, samples and search budget. A null graph result is a null result.

## Non-blocking, but settle before claiming an end-task edge

- **Budget and objective:** `wg3_oracle.py` gets its whole-episode churn cap from a noarb run on the same future tape. That is fine for a privileged diagnostic but unavailable online. Specify an observable, causal churn allowance/schedule and run every comparator under a comparable constraint. Hard constraints and xApp goal retention need *episode-level* validation; a short-horizon energy/LL price does not implement them. Verify `TrueSimWM` clones between propose/apply, scores the pending batch, and uses exactly the learned planner's candidates/tie-breaks; preserve a persistent equivalence test rather than deleting it.
- **Keep/retire:** Preserve `sequence.py`, E-series learned/rollout tools and the legacy baselines for reproducibility until dependency and baseline fidelity audits pass. A new E6 QACM *mapping* is not automatically faithful to the published method; `published.py` explicitly has interface-only entries and declared resolver substitutions. Plan 011 is a historical, legacy-qualified decision; update it and plan 012 deliberately before any hard deletion. Lazy imports are a reasonable seam fix, not proof retirement is complete.

**Build order:** (1) pass E6 scenario/controllability and corrected WG3 oracle gates; (2) freeze the policy estimand, observability, budget and trace schema; (3) implement/log and verify a compact TrueSim reference plus short-horizon-vs-90 s ranking test; (4) collect randomized *joint-policy* training episodes; (5) train/evaluate response models and calibrated gate on held-out oracle panels against accept-all, all-input and SHAP baselines; (6) only then closed-loop tests on untouched regimes and published comparators; (7) clean up legacy dependencies. Drop the arbitrary Spearman >0.5 gate: require lower paired **decision regret** than accept-all with credible uncertainty and no guardrail failures. If not, stop searching and fix data/target before invoking MSCR as the differentiator.
